### Description

Bread Wallet by Miden is the official self-custodial wallet for the Miden network.

Self-custody by default
Your wallet keys are generated on your device and kept there in encrypted storage. No account or sign-up is required.

Choose how you pay
Send and receive Miden assets, with public or private transfer behavior available where the selected flow supports it.

Recovery that keeps you in control
Your recovery phrase restores key ownership. Guardian accounts can add recovery and private-state continuity through your selected operator, while neither your device nor the Guardian can spend alone.

Open source
Bread Wallet is open source. Review the code and follow development on GitHub.

Made for iPhone

- Unlock with Face ID or your device passcode.
- Share an address by QR code and choose recall timing for supported transfers.
- Browse Miden dApps inside the wallet and review requests before confirming them.
- Choose a Guardian operator when creating a Guardian account.
